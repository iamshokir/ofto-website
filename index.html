How we work

From a model on a laptop to light in a classroom.

  1. Research

    We study how cold, snow and outages affect solar power and batteries at schools.

  2. Design

    We size solar, storage and backup for a specific school building and its winter load.

  3. Pilot

    We are preparing our first pilot at a mountain school in Tajikistan.

  4. Share

    We publish the models, code and data so anyone can check them or reuse them.

Whether you give your time, your skills or your support, every step brings light to one more classroom.
