Get involved

Researchers. If you work on cold-climate storage, rural electrification or resilience planning and want to compare results or work together, please write.

Volunteers. Students and early-career engineers who want to help with data, modelling or literature reviews are welcome to introduce themselves.

Schools and institutions. Schools, districts and ministries can ask whether this sizing work transfers to their own buildings, and what it would take to find out. Institutions interested in supporting the research are welcome too.

The simplest way is an email to shokir@oftoenergy.org with a few lines about who you are and what you have in mind.

Donate

Donations go toward our first pilot: a solar-plus-storage system for a mountain school in Tajikistan.
