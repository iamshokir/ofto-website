What has happened so far.

Short notes on the foundation’s work, newest first.

  • November 2026

    Conference paper for IEEE PES General Meeting 2027

    A short paper on cold-morning heating peaks in electrified New York State schools was sent to the IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting 2027. Code: github.com/iamshokir/school-peak-ny.

  • October 2026

    Mountain-school microgrid study sent to Energy for Sustainable Development

    Where to place the battery in a PV–battery–diesel microgrid for a remote school in Tajikistan, and whether it stays worth installing. Code and data: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23283637.

  • 2026

    Founder named IEEE YESIST12 2026 Ambassador

    Shokirdzhon Ismoilov serves as an ambassador for IEEE YESIST12 (Youth Endeavors for Social Innovation using Sustainable Technology).

  • 2026

    Volunteer researchers join

    Researchers from Tajikistan, Russia, Turkey and India begin working with the foundation on the school-energy studies.

  • 2025

    IEEE Day 2025 Ambassador

    The founder served as an IEEE Day 2025 Ambassador.

  • 2025

    The initiative begins

    Work starts on one question: how a school can keep its power on through winter when the grid fails.
