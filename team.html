Founder

Shokirdzhon Ismoilov

Founder and Principal Investigator

A power engineer by training and by trade. He graduated from the Dushanbe branch of MPEI in 2019; his diploma project sized renewable supply for a village without reliable power.

He then worked at the 220 kV substation of the Sangtuda-1 hydropower plant, and afterwards at Barqi Tojik, the national utility, on procurement, coordination of energy projects, and applied research on smart metering and network modernization. The school question came out of that work.

At OFTO he sets the research questions and study design, sizes PV systems for schools, reviews AI and data approaches to grid monitoring and planning, and mentors and reviews the work of the volunteer researchers.
